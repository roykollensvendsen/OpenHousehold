# ADR-003 — canonical accounts and explicit sharing

Status: Proposed, 2026-09-20. Decision owners: both adults and maintainer.

Context: one account may appear under two separate consents. Summing provider
records can double money. Bank authorization does not imply household disclosure.

Proposal: one canonical account, evidence-backed external aliases, independent
access and sharing grants, exact money and versioned source observations. Full
booked balances of selected shared NOK accounts fund the single active goal.
Changes to the funding set reset comparisons; manual notes never overwrite bank
facts. Private information is excluded from all shared derivatives.

Alternatives: share everything (simpler but broader disclosure), aggregate-only
sharing (additional policy complexity), budget-envelope goal allocations (more
flexible but needs allocation and reconciliation rules). The proposed full-account
model is easy to explain but needs dedicated accounts. Q-01/Q-02 must approve
these tradeoffs, including withdrawal and joint deletion behavior.
